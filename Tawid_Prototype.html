Tawid — check it, pay it, track it

Simulated proof of concept. A customer in Australia pays a freelancer in the Philippines through one pay link. Tawid first checks that the payment can get through and fixes the price, then the customer pays at their own provider, then both sides track the same record. If it fails, Tawid says what happened, who must act and where the money is. People and providers shown here are made up.

    What this page does and does not show

    • Works here: the check before paying, the order of Open Payments calls, consent, a hash check on the approval, price expiry, single use of a price, the shared tracking record and the failure dictionary.
    • Simulated: both providers, their authorisation and resource servers, the Interledger packets, the exchange rate and all balances. They run inside this page.
    • Separate script, not this page: the same flow written with the official Open Payments SDK and signed requests. It has been dry-run against a local stand-in only.
    • Not done: a payment on the Interledger test network, real providers, ID checks, settlement between providers, and partly delivered payments.
